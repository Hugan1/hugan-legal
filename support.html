Hugan Support

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Hugan is a local-first cookie-cutter catalogue app for bakers and studios. Your cutter library, photos, order sets, catalogue exports, and catalogue-run balance stay on this device. There is no Hugan account and no Hugan cloud sync.

Please include your iOS version, the screen you were on, and what happened. Do not attach personal photos or private details you do not want shared.

Getting started

  • Tap Get Started on the welcome screen to enter the Shelf tab. This only finishes onboarding; it does not create an account.
  • Add or edit cutter cards with photos, shape, size, maker, theme tags, bin location, and notes.
  • Build order sets to see which shapes you already have and which are missing.
  • Use Studio to preview and export a catalogue page image. Each successful export spends 1 catalogue run.

Permissions

Hugan asks for system permissions only when you use the related control:

  • Camera — take a photo for a cutter card
  • Photo Library — import a photo or save a finished catalogue image
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — dictate a short baking note (you can type instead)

If a permission was denied, open iOS Settings → Hugan and turn it back on.

Catalogue gifts (In-App Purchase)

Studio Gifts sells consumable catalogue-run packs through Apple In-App Purchase. These packs unlock digital catalogue exports inside the app only.

Hugan does not sell physical goods or real-world services through In-App Purchase. Catalogue gifts cannot be used to buy cookie cutters, bakery products, shipping, delivery, or any offline service.

Verified purchases increase the local run balance. Cancelled, failed, pending, or unverified purchases do not. Because packs are consumable and stored on this device, Hugan does not offer a Restore Purchases button. Unused balance can be lost if you clear all data or remove the app. For billing or refund questions, contact Apple Support.

Log Out and Clear All Data

  • Log Out returns to the welcome screen and keeps local data.
  • Clear All Data permanently deletes cutters, sets, exports, purchases, and remaining runs on this install.
