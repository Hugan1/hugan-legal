Hugan Privacy Policy

Hugan is a personal cookie-cutter catalogue app for bakers and studios. This Privacy Policy explains how Hugan handles information when you use the app on your device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Hugan mobile application. Hugan is designed as a local-first tool: your cutter library, photos, sets, and catalogue exports stay on the device where you install the app unless you choose to save an image to your photo library or complete an Apple in-app purchase.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Hugan, the app may store:

  • Cutter card details you enter, such as name, shape, size, maker, purchase year, theme tags, bin or shelf location, and free-text notes
  • Photos you capture with the camera or import from your photo library for cutter cards and catalogue pages
  • Order sets you build, including required shapes and missing-piece hints
  • Draft catalogue previews, finished export records, and your local catalogue-run balance
  • Purchase records needed for local idempotency after Apple confirms a consumable transaction
  • Lightweight preferences such as whether onboarding is complete

Clearing all data inside Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes this on-device content according to iOS storage rules.

Permissions and when they appear

Hugan requests system permissions only when you start the related action:

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo to photograph a cutter for a catalogue card
  • Photo Library — when you choose an existing photo or save a finished catalogue image
  • Microphone — when you tap Record Note to capture a short spoken note about a cutter
  • Speech Recognition — when Hugan converts that spoken note into editable text

Hugan does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not implement advertising tracking inside the app. Hugan also does not claim the notification center delegate on this package surface.

Purchases

Consumable catalogue gifts are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment information under Apple's terms and privacy practices. Hugan stores verified transaction identifiers and granted catalogue runs locally so the same transaction is not credited twice. Consumable balances remain on the device and are not restored after deletion or a full data clear.

Network and third parties

Opening Privacy Policy or Terms of Use inside Hugan loads these legal pages over HTTPS. The app does not operate a Hugan account backend for syncing your cutter library. If you leave Hugan to use Apple services, those services are governed by Apple.

Children

Hugan is intended for adult bakers and studio operators who manage cutter inventory. Do not provide the app for unsupervised use by children if that conflicts with your local rules.

Your choices

  • Deny a permission in iOS Settings if you no longer want camera, photos, microphone, or speech access
  • Use Clear All Data to erase local cutters, media references, sets, exports, purchases, and remaining balance on this install
  • Use Log Out to return to the guide screen without necessarily wiping data, or clear data when you want a full reset

Contact
